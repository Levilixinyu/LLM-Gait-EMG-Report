# Message to the third independent reviewer

Dear Reviewer,

We would like to invite you, as a sports medicine expert, to independently review 105 anonymized gait/EMG interpretation reports covering 25 stroke cases and 10 healthy controls. You are joining at the scoring stage; you are not being asked to develop the criteria or confirm any earlier reviewer's judgments.

Please use the attached **Formal_review_rater3.zip**. Extract the entire archive and open **rater3/START_HERE.html**. Everything needed for scoring is included and works offline: a reviewer orientation, the approved rubric and error definitions, 42 teaching examples, a blank workbook, and each report's exact model inputs, case requirements, reference card and curves.

Please proceed as follows:

1. Read **ORIENTATION.html**, **HOW_TO_REVIEW.html**, the full rubric, error definitions and teaching examples. The orientation explains the limited-evidence interpretation task and the meaning of the supplied metrics. Historical draft labels are explained by the included approval status; the Coverage sheet contains the current case-specific requirements.
2. Complete a separate copy of **REVIEWER_BACKGROUND.md**, including your relevant gait/EMG experience and any prior exposure or conflicts.
3. Save the blank workbook as **Independent_ratings_rater3_completed.xlsx**. Follow the fixed report order across sessions. For each report, complete its Coverage rows, record any distinct issues with exact continuous quotations, and finish its Review row.
4. Judge the report against the information actually supplied to the model. The expert curves help check evidence but do not create additional mandatory content. Keep coverage and errors separate, and use Pending with reasons when an interpretation needs later discussion.
5. After all reports are reviewed, enter your name, completion date as ISO text and Yes in the workbook's completion fields. Return the completed workbook and **Reviewer_background_rater3_completed.md**.

Please work independently without viewing other reviewers' ratings or discussing report judgments before the coordinator opens the discussion stage. Do not try to identify the models. If identity information becomes known, record the circumstance. If a rule or file is unclear, contact the coordinator for a documented clarification; no extra statistical calculations or new scoring criteria are required from you.

Please take breaks as needed and keep the fixed order when resuming. Your independent opinions, including uncertainties or concerns about the materials, are welcome.

Thank you.
