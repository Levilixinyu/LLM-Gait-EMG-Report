EMG findings are derived from ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to their own peak available values. "Above-threshold" indicates regions where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle, not absolute neural on/off times or force measurements. The analysis cycle runs from paretic initial contact (0%) to the subsequent initial contact (100%).
