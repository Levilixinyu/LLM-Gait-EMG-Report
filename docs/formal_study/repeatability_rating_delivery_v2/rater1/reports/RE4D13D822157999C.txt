**1. Objective Findings**
Participant Sub22_P (stroke group) was evaluated on the paretic side. The participant’s self-selected gait speed was 0.45 m/s (categorized within the limited community ambulator band), with a cadence of 78.8 steps/min. Step length symmetry, calculated using the successive-footfall definition, was 5.7%. 

Because the self-selected gait speed of 0.45 m/s falls below the slowest observed speed in the healthy reference cohort (0.80 m/s), no speed-matched reference data are available in this dataset. As a result, all comparisons to the reference cohort conflate the biomechanical effects of slow walking with stroke-specific control changes, and the two factors cannot be disentangled from these data alone.

**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG interval detection is based on an averaged envelope exceeding 20% of its own peak for a minimum of 3% of the cycle. Normalized amplitudes represent relative envelope peaks, not absolute force or stride-to-stride variability. Gait cycle boundaries occur at 0% and 100% (successive initial contacts). 

Detected above-threshold intervals and normalized peaks are as follows:
*   **GAS**: Activity detected from 0%-13%, 14%-19%, and 31%-64% of the cycle (60% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.61.
*   **RF**: Activity detected from 88%-53%, maintaining continuous above-threshold activity across the initial contact boundary (68% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.45.
*   **VL**: Activity detected from 69%-81% and 91%-54%, wrapping across the cycle boundary (75% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.59.
*   **BF**: Activity detected from 59%-38%, wrapping across the cycle boundary (80% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.46.
*   **ST**: Activity detected from 62%-35%, wrapping across the cycle boundary (73% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.38.
*   **TA**: Activity detected from 61%-37%, wrapping across the cycle boundary (77% total coverage); peak normalized amplitude 0.50.
*   **ERS**: Activity detected from 0%-100%, indicating 100% total coverage with no below-threshold segment in the averaged envelope; peak normalized amplitude 0.64.

Co-contraction indices (CCI) were calculated over the full continuous envelopes (0-100%):
*   **TA vs GAS**: CCI is 0.56. This falls within the supplied empirical healthy reference interval (2.5th-97.5th percentile: 0.24-0.59) and is above the reference median (0.39).
*   **RF vs BF**: CCI is 0.71. This is near the healthy reference median (0.68) and falls within the reference interval (0.48-0.83). 
*Note: CCI falling within the empirical reference interval depends heavily on normalization and algorithm parameters and does not, by itself, establish normal muscle function or physiological coordination.*
