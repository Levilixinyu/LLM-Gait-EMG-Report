**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG findings reflect ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to a shared maximum across available strides for each muscle. The metrics below do not represent absolute muscular force, strength, or neural activation, and the average curves do not establish stride-to-stride variability. Above-threshold intervals denote regions where the averaged envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for a minimum of 3% of the cycle. Retained intervals are identified solely by these threshold and duration rules.

Co-contraction indices (CCI) compute the whole-cycle overlap of continuous normalized EMG envelopes and can include sub-threshold activity. The TA vs. GAS CCI is 0.38, which is within the healthy reference dataset interval of 0.24-0.59 and proximately aligned with the median of 0.39. The RF vs. BF CCI is 0.73, which falls within the supplied reference interval of 0.48-0.83. Placement inside these empirical intervals does not establish physiologically normal co-activation, just as placement outside them would not establish pathological co-activation or impaired control.
