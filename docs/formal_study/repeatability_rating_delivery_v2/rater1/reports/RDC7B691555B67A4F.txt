These findings describe the **averaged envelopes under the stated detection rule**. They do not establish physiological onset, offset, contraction duration, continuous contraction, inactivity outside retained intervals, or the same pattern in every stride. Without intermediate events and matched timing references, intervals cannot be assigned gait-phase functions or characterized as early, delayed, or prolonged.

Maximum absolute z locates the largest standardized difference from the reference—not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at that location. These curvewise extrema should not be classified using a conventional 2-SD rule. ERS 6.5 exceeds its supplied pooled healthy 95th percentile of 3.65; TA 3.8 is below its supplied percentile of 4.61. Other muscle-specific percentiles are not supplied here.
