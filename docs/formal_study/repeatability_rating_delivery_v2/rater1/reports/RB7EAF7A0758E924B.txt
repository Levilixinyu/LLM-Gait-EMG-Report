At every listed maximum-|z| location, the envelope is **above the phase-specific reference mean**. These locations identify the largest standardized reference differences, not necessarily amplitude peaks or gait events.

**Whole-cycle CCI**
- **TA–GAS: 0.67**, above the supplied empirical interval of 0.24–0.59; reference median 0.39.
- **RF–BF: 0.75**, within the supplied interval of 0.48–0.83 and above its median of 0.68.

CCI measures whole-cycle overlap of normalized continuous envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, force or a phase-specific result. These comparisons do not establish normal or pathological co-activation, and their interpretation depends on the normalization and envelope methods used.

EMG maximum-|z| values are extreme-value statistics across 1001 cycle points, so a conventional 2-SD abnormality rule is inappropriate. ERS 5.1 exceeds its supplied pooled healthy 95th percentile of 3.65; TA 3.3 is below its corresponding value of 4.61. Exact muscle-specific percentiles for the other recorded muscles are not supplied.

**Reference limitations:** The stated deviation reference comprises 138 pooled healthy subjects. This is not a matched comparison, and 0.36 m/s is below the healthy cohort’s 0.80 m/s speed floor; no speed-matched healthy reference exists in this dataset. Slow-walking effects and stroke-specific effects therefore cannot be separated here. Pooled calibration percentiles are not matched leave-one-out normal limits, and these comparisons cannot establish a disease effect or clinical diagnosis.
