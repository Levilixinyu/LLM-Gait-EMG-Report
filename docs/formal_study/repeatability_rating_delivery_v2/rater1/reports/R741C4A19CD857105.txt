Maximum |z| identifies the largest standardized difference from the phase-specific reference mean—not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” in the table refer to that mean, not a percentile limit. **Whole-cycle CCI comparisons:**
- **TA–GAS: 0.38**, within the supplied empirical interval **0.24–0.59**, and slightly below its median **0.39**.
- **RF–BF: 0.73**, within **0.48–0.83**, and above its median **0.68**.

CCI measures whole-cycle overlap of normalized continuous envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, force or a phase-specific result. Its values depend on normalization and envelope methods; being within these intervals does not establish physiologically normal co-activation or muscle function.
