All seven mean envelopes are explicitly reported as remaining above threshold across the cycle boundary. The analysis window extends from paretic initial contact at 0% to the next initial contact at 100%. ERS additionally has an explicitly supplied full-window interval, supporting above-threshold coverage throughout the analyzed mean envelope, rather than relying on rounded coverage alone.

These intervals do not establish physiological onset, offset, continuous contraction, or inactivity elsewhere, and need not occur identically in every stride. Intermediate gait events and matched muscle-specific timing references are unavailable; therefore, intervals cannot be assigned gait phases or characterized as early, delayed, or prolonged.

**Whole-cycle co-contraction indices**

- **TA–GAS: 0.62**, above the supplied empirical interval of 0.24–0.59 and median of 0.39.
- **RF–BF: 0.68**, equal to the supplied median of 0.68 and within the empirical interval of 0.48–0.83.

These Falconer–Winter CCI values quantify whole-cycle overlap of continuous normalized envelopes, including subthreshold contributions. They are not simultaneous activation times, force estimates, or phase-specific findings. Their interpretation depends on the normalization and envelope methods; neither result establishes physiological normality, pathological co-activation, stiffness, or impaired control.

Comparability is limited: the participant’s speed of 0.21 m/s is below the healthy cohort’s 0.80–1.79 m/s range, so no speed-matched healthy reference exists in this dataset.
