**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG parameters reflect averaged envelopes relative to each muscle's maximum amplitude across available strides. They do not represent absolute muscle strength or individual stride-to-stride variability. Above-threshold intervals are reported as a percentage of the gait cycle (0% to 100%, from initial contact to next initial contact) where the envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle. 

*   **GAS:** 70% of the cycle was above threshold. Intervals were detected at 73%-82% and wrapping across the cycle boundary from 97% to 57%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.55. The maximum |z| deviation from the healthy reference was 8.1 (above the phase-specific reference mean at 2% of the cycle).
*   **RF:** 97% of the cycle was above threshold. The detected interval wrapped the cycle boundary from 97% to 91%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.42. The maximum |z| deviation was 2.6 (above reference at 29%).
*   **VL:** 52% of the cycle was above threshold. The interval wrapped the cycle boundary from 99% to 50%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.45. The maximum |z| deviation was 4.5 (above reference at 28%).
*   **BF:** 100% of the cycle was above threshold, recorded continuously from 0%-100%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.53. The maximum |z| deviation was 3.9 (above reference at 19%).
*   **ST:** 95% of the cycle was above threshold. The detected interval wrapped the cycle boundary from 65% to 57%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.56. The maximum |z| deviation was 3.5 (above reference at 49%).
*   **TA:** 99% of the cycle was above threshold. The detected interval wrapped the cycle boundary from 48% to 48%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.56. The maximum |z| deviation was 2.7 (above reference at 35%).
*   **ERS:** 100% of the cycle was above threshold, recorded continuously from 0%-100%. Peak normalized amplitude was 0.63. The maximum |z| deviation was 5.3 (above reference at 81%).
*   *Note:* Every evaluated muscle demonstrated above-threshold activity crossing the initial contact cycle boundary.

*Co-contraction Indices (CCI):*
CCI represents a whole-cycle overlap index of the continuous normalized EMG envelopes. Being outside an empirical reference interval does not inherently establish pathological control, nor does it quantify simultaneous force.
*   **TA vs. GAS:** CCI was 0.72. This falls above the supplied healthy cohort median (0.39) and reference interval (2.5th–97.5th percentile: 0.24–0.59).
*   **RF vs. BF:** CCI was 0.83. This aligns with the upper endpoint of the supplied healthy cohort reference interval (2.5th–97.5th percentile: 0.48–0.83; median 0.68).
