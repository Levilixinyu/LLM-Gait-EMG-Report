Normalization used a participant-, side-, and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, without additional normalization of the released mean curves. Peak values are relative envelope amplitudes—not strength, force, or a basis for ranking muscles as stronger or weaker.

Maximum |z| identifies the largest standardized difference across 1001 cycle points, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean. Its magnitude depends on normalization and envelope methods; these comparisons do not establish normal muscle function or pathological co-activation.
