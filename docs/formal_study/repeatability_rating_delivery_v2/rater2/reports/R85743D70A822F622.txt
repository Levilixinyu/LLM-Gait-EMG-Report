RF, VL, BF, ST, TA and ERS explicitly retain above-threshold continuity across the cycle-defining **initial-contact boundary**. The 0% and 100% endpoints represent successive initial contacts. Peak amplitudes are relative envelope values, not strength or force. The maximum |z| locations identify the largest standardized reference differences, not necessarily envelope peaks or gait events. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at that location.
