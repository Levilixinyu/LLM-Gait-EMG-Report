**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
Measurements in this section are based on ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to their own peak across available strides. Detected intervals reflect regions where the mean envelope meets the 20% amplitude and 3% duration threshold rules; they do not establish continuous contraction, physiological onset/offset times, complete inactivity outside the intervals, or stride-to-stride variability. Relative envelope amplitudes do not represent absolute muscular force or strength.

**Co-Contraction Indices (CCI):**
CCI represents a whole-cycle overlap index of the normalized EMG envelopes, capturing both above- and below-threshold mean signal contributions. These indices depend on the algorithm and normalization methods used. Being outside or inside an empirical reference interval does not by itself establish pathological stiffness, impaired control, or physiologically normal muscle function.
