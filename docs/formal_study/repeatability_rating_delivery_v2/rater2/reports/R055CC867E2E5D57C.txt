All seven mean envelopes are explicitly reported above threshold across the initial-contact cycle boundary. “Wrap” denotes continuity through the 100%→0% boundary. These results do **not** establish physiological onset or offset, continuous contraction, inactivity outside retained intervals, or the same pattern in every stride.

Normalization uses a shared maximum across available strides for each participant, side and muscle, before averaging; the denominator is not shared between sides. No additional amplitude normalization was applied here. Peak values therefore represent relative envelope amplitude—not strength, force or absolute neural activation.

At each listed maximum-|z| location, the envelope is **above the phase-specific healthy-reference mean**. These locations identify the largest standardized reference difference, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event.
