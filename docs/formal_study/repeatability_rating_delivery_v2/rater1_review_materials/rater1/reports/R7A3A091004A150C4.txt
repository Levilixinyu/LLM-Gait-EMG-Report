## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

The retained intervals describe where the **ensemble-averaged envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak**, subject to a minimum interval length of 3% of the cycle by sample count. They are not physiological onset/offset measurements or evidence of continuous contraction. Segments outside retained intervals cannot be interpreted as inactivity.

“Wrap” denotes continuity through the 100%→0% analysis-cycle boundary. All seven mean envelopes are explicitly reported as above threshold across the initial-contact boundary. ERS has a retained interval spanning the full analysis window; GAS and ST each have reported coverage of 99%. These findings do not establish the same pattern in every stride, and rounded coverage alone does not establish the absence of below-threshold segments.

Normalization used a participant-, side- and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, without further amplitude normalization here. Thus, these amplitudes are relative envelope values—not strength, force or a basis for ranking muscle strength.

Maximum |z| identifies the largest standardized reference difference across 1001 cycle points, **not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event**. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at the listed location, not to a healthy percentile boundary.
