These intervals do not establish physiological onset or offset, continuous contraction, inactivity outside the intervals, or the same pattern in every stride. Without individual events or matched muscle-specific timing references, they cannot be assigned gait-phase functions or characterized as normal, early, delayed or prolonged.

Maximum |z| identifies the largest standardized difference across the cycle, **not necessarily peak envelope amplitude**. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at the listed location. TA **5.0** exceeds the supplied pooled healthy 95th percentile of **4.61**, and ERS **6.3** exceeds **3.65**. Muscle-specific percentile limits are not supplied for the other muscles. These extreme-value statistics should not be classified using a conventional |z| > 2 rule.
