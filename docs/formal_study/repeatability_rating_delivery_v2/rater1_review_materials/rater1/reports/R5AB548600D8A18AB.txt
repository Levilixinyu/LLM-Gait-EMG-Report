**1. Objective Findings**
The provided data apply to the paretic limb of a participant in the stroke group (Sub27_P). 
- Gait speed was measured at 0.75 m/s. While this falls into a classification band designated "limited community ambulator," this label denotes a speed range and does not measure true real-world mobility, independence, or safety. 
- Cadence was 82.5 steps/minute. 
- Step length symmetry index (successive-footfall definition) was -15.8%, indicating that the paretic-side step is shorter than the non-paretic step.

*Data Limitation Note:* The participant's gait speed of 0.75 m/s falls below the slowest self-selected walking speed (0.80 m/s) of the healthy reference cohort. Consequently, no speed-matched healthy reference exists in this dataset. Any observed deviations conflate the effects of slow walking with stroke-specific pathology, and these factors cannot be isolated from the supplied data. 

**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
The following findings describe the mean EMG envelopes across the recorded strides. Intervals reflect where the averaged, normalized envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the analysis cycle. These algorithms do not document single-stride variability, true physiological onset/offset, continuous contraction, or inactivity outside the intervals. The cycle runs from initial contact (0%) to subsequent initial contact (100%).

- **Gastrocnemius (GAS):** Above-threshold intervals occurred at 22%-28%, 36%-66%, and 99%-21% (wrapping across the initial contact boundary). Total coverage was 58% of the cycle, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.55.

*Co-Contraction Indices (CCI):*
CCI represents a whole-cycle overlap index of the continuous normalized envelopes. 
- TA vs. GAS: CCI = 0.40. This value sits near the reference median of 0.39 and falls within the supplied 2.5th-97.5th percentile interval (0.24-0.59).
- RF vs. BF: CCI = 0.54. This value falls below the reference median of 0.68, though it remains within the supplied empirical interval (0.48-0.83).
*Note: Falling inside or outside these reference intervals does not independently establish physiologically normal co-activation, pathological stiffness, or normal muscle function.*
