EMG parameters reflect properties of ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to a shared maximum across available strides for each specific muscle. Detected intervals represent regions where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle, and do not represent physiological onset, continuous single-stride contraction, or absolute neural force. Portions of the cycle outside these intervals do not establish complete muscle inactivity.
