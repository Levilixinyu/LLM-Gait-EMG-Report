All seven envelopes are reported above threshold across the cycle boundary. The analysis cycle extends from paretic initial contact at **0%** to the next paretic initial contact at **100%**. GAS and BF have full-window retained intervals.

These are properties of averaged envelopes under the stated detection rule—not physiological onset, offset, continuous contraction, or evidence of inactivity elsewhere. They do not establish the same pattern in every stride. Individual toe-off and matched muscle-specific timing references are not supplied, so gait-phase functions and normal/early/delayed timing cannot be assigned.

Maximum |z| identifies the largest standardized reference difference across **1001 cycle points**, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above reference” refers to the reference mean at that particular cycle location. The supplied pooled healthy 95th-percentile calibration value is **3.65** for ERS, below the reported **4.8**. TA **4.6** is close to its supplied value of **4.61**; the displayed precision does not support a boundary classification. These extreme-value statistics should not be interpreted using a conventional 2-SD abnormality rule.

**Whole-cycle CCI:**
- **TA–GAS: 0.78**, above the supplied empirical interval **0.24–0.59** and median **0.39**.
- **RF–BF: 0.65**, within **0.48–0.83** and below the supplied median **0.68**.

CCI measures overlap of the full normalized envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is method-dependent and is not simultaneous activation time, force, or a phase-specific result. These comparisons establish neither pathological nor physiologically normal co-activation or muscle function.
