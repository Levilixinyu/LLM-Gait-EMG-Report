GVS and GPS-like values summarize waveform differences from the reference; they do not identify their direction or cycle location. The ROM values alone do not establish clinical restriction without an appropriate comparison.

**Reference limitations apply throughout this report:** the stated deviation reference comprises **138 pooled healthy subjects**, evaluated at **1001 phase points**, from **main_cohort_excel**. At **0.21 m/s**, this participant walks below the healthy cohort’s **0.80–1.79 m/s** range, so no speed-matched healthy reference exists in this dataset. Reference differences may reflect slow-walking effects as well as stroke-related or other participant-specific factors; these cannot be separated here. Pooled calibration percentiles are not matched leave-one-out normal limits. These comparisons therefore cannot isolate a disease effect or establish a clinical diagnosis.
