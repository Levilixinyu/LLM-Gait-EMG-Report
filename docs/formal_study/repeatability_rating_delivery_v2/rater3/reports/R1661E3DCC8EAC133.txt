## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

These are **ensemble-averaged envelope** findings. Normalization uses a participant-, side- and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, without further amplitude normalization here. Peak values therefore represent relative envelope amplitude, not force, strength or absolute neural activation.

These intervals do not identify physiological onset, offset or contraction duration, and regions outside retained intervals do not establish inactivity. The average curves also do not establish that the same pattern occurred in every stride. No intermediate gait events are supplied, so the intervals cannot be assigned stance/swing or phase-specific functions.

These locations identify the largest standardized reference differences, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. **Whole-cycle CCI:**

CCI quantifies whole-cycle overlap of normalized continuous envelopes, including subthreshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, force or a phase-specific result. These comparisons do not establish pathological or physiologically normal co-activation; values depend on the normalization and envelope method.
