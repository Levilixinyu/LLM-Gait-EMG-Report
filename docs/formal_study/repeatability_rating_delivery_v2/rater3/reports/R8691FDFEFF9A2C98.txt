## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

The retained intervals describe where the **ensemble-averaged envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak**, with a minimum retained interval of **3% of the cycle by sample count**. They are not physiological onset/offset measurements or evidence of continuous contraction, inactivity outside retained intervals, or an identical pattern in every stride.

Normalization uses a participant-, side-, and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, without further amplitude normalization here. Peak values are relative envelope amplitudes, not measures of force or strength.
