**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG findings are based on averaged envelopes for each muscle across contributing strides. Above-threshold intervals indicate regions where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its own maximum and is maintained for at least 3% of the analysis cycle. The cycle is defined from 0% to 100% between consecutive initial contacts of the contributing strides; no intermediate physiological events (such as toe-off) are identified. Note that a lack of a detected interval does not mean the muscle is completely inactive, and these intervals do not establish continuous contraction in individual strides.
