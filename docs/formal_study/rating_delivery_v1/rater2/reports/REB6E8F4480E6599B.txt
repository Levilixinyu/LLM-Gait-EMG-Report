**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG findings describe features of the averaged envelopes based on a 20% relative amplitude threshold and a 3% minimum duration rule. Peak normalized amplitudes reflect relative envelope values under this specific processing pipeline, not absolute muscular force, strength, or individual stride-to-stride variability.

*   **Boundary Continuity:** The averaged envelopes for RF, VL, BF, ST, TA, and ERS all retain above-threshold intervals that wrap across the cycle-defining boundary (initial contact). GAS does not retain an above-threshold interval crossing this boundary.

    *   *Note:* Falling within an empirical reference interval does not establish physiologically normal co-activation or muscle function.
