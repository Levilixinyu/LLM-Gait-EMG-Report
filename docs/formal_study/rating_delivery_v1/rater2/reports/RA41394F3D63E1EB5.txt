All seven mean envelopes are explicitly reported above threshold across the initial-contact cycle boundary. ERS has a retained interval spanning the complete analysis window. These findings do not establish physiological onset, offset, continuous contraction, inactivity outside retained intervals, or the same pattern in every stride. Without individual events and matched muscle-specific timing references, timing cannot be classified as normal, early, delayed, or prolonged.

Maximum |z| identifies the **largest standardized reference difference**, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific healthy reference mean. GAS has the largest listed value, 6.5. TA 3.4 and ERS 2.2 are below their supplied pooled healthy 95th-percentile calibration values of 4.61 and 3.65, respectively; these comparisons do not establish normal muscle physiology. The conventional |z| > 2 rule is inappropriate for this cycle-wide extreme statistic.

The **GPS-like score is 6.29**, above the supplied calibration median of 5.1 and below its 95th percentile of 10.7. This is an RMS summary across the supplied sagittal channels, not the validated nine-variable GPS or a clinical severity grade.

The deviation reference actually used comprised **138 pooled healthy subjects**, evaluated over **1001 phase points** from `main_cohort_excel`.
