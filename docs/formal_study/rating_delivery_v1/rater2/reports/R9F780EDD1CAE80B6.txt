Normalization uses a participant-, side-, and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, without further amplitude normalization. These relative amplitudes do not measure strength or force and cannot rank muscles as stronger or weaker.

The conventional |z| > 2 rule is inappropriate for these extreme-value statistics. TA’s 4.2 is below the supplied pooled 95th percentile of 4.61; ERS’s 4.2 is above the supplied 3.65.
