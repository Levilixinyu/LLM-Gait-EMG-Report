**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
Measurements represent averaged envelopes normalized to their own peak within the available strides. Above-threshold intervals indicate portions of the cycle where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak (minimum 3% duration by sample count). These intervals cross the 100%-to-0% analysis boundary (initial contact of the paretic limb) when the first percentage is greater than the second. These features describe mean envelope properties, not physiological onsets, muscle forces, or individual stride-to-stride variability.
