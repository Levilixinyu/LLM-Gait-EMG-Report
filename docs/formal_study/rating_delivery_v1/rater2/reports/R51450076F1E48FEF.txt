**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG findings describe ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to their own peak across available strides. Intervals denote regions where the mean curve exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle. These amplitudes represent relative envelope values, not muscular force, strength, or absolute neural activation, and average curves do not convey stride-to-stride variability. 

*   **Envelope Coverage and Boundary Behavior**: 
    *   Rectus Femoris (RF) and Erector Spinae (ERS) maintained above-threshold amplitude for 100% of the gait cycle, remaining above threshold across the initial contact boundary.
    *   Vastus Lateralis (VL) and Semitendinosus (ST) demonstrated 89% and 87% cycle coverage, respectively. Both remained above threshold across the initial contact boundary, with intervals of 87%-73% for VL and 86%-74% for ST.
    *   Gastrocnemius (GAS) showed 75% coverage with intervals at 5%-74% and 99%-4%, wrapping across the cycle boundary.
