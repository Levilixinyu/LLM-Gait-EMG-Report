**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
The following findings are derived from ensemble-averaged, amplitude-normalized EMG envelopes. Detected intervals indicate where the envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for a minimum of 3% of the cycle, anchored from the initial contact of contributing strides (0%). These intervals describe the thresholded average signal; they do not determine single-stride physiological onset/offset, continuous contraction, or absolute muscular force.

CCI values measure the overlap of the full, normalized continuous envelopes (including sub-threshold activity) and do not represent absolute simultaneous force. Being within the empirical reference range does not independently establish physiologically normal muscle function.
