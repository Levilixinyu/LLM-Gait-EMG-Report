**Whole-cycle CCI:**

CCI measures whole-cycle overlap of continuous normalized envelopes, including sub-threshold values. It is not simultaneous activation time, muscular force, or a phase-specific result, and it depends on normalization and envelope processing. Neither comparison establishes pathological or physiologically normal muscle function.

Slow-walking effects and stroke-associated differences cannot be separated here.
