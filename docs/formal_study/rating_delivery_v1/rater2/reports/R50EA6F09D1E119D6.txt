These findings concern the mean envelopes, not continuous physiological contraction or a pattern established in every stride. Outside retained intervals, physiological inactivity cannot be inferred. No intermediate gait events are supplied, so intervals cannot be assigned to stance, swing, or phase-specific functions.

Normalization used a shared maximum across available strides before averaging, without additional amplitude normalization here. Peak values are relative envelope amplitudes—not force, strength, or absolute neural activation—and do not establish stride-to-stride variability.

Maximum \|z\| identifies the largest standardized reference difference, **not necessarily the envelope-amplitude peak**. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean. These cycle-wide extreme statistics should not be classified using a conventional 2-SD rule.
