Independent review: rater2

105 reports, 35 cases, 552 coverage judgments. Use your own packet and follow its fixed order across sessions. Do not compare ratings with the other reviewer until both independent reviews are locked.

1. Start with the workbook and report list

Extract the whole ZIP first. Open START_HERE.html in a browser and Independent_ratings.xlsx in Excel or another spreadsheet editor that supports dropdown lists. The reading pages work offline. Record all judgments in the workbook; the browser pages do not save ratings.

Save your working copy as Independent_ratings_rater2_completed.xlsx. Keep the delivered template unchanged. Do not rename sheets, columns, report IDs or requirements, and do not sort the review order.

2. Read one report with its evidence

Open reports in the numbered list order. Each page shows the exact report, its case requirements, links to the exact model inputs, the reference card and the EMG/kinematic curves. The model received the saved system and user text, not the expert curves. Do not require observations that were visible only in the expert plots.

The approved rubric retains historical draft wording and a five-case developmental annex. Its later approval is documented in the approval status. For these 35 cases, use the requirements already populated in Coverage, including G03 once per report. Read the current review scope.

3. Complete Coverage

For the current report ID, fill columns F-H. The reading page identifies the corresponding spreadsheet rows.

ColumnWhat to enter
F: decisionCovered, Partially covered or Not covered. Use the substantive meaning and AND/OR branches, not word matching. Cannot assess is for missing, corrupt or mismatched review materials, not a cautious or poor report.
G: adjudication_statusFinal when your independent judgment is settled; Pending when a reading remains unresolved. Final here is your own judgment, not agreement with the other reviewer.
H: evidence_and_reasonRecord supporting evidence and the missing/wrong component where relevant. For Pending, explain the competing readings; a provisional category is permitted and a final category need not be forced.

Coverage and errors are separate: a correct required statement may earn coverage even if an unretracted contradiction elsewhere also needs an issue row. Do not convert partial coverage into a numerical score. G03 requires reference-comparability and inference limits; age and speed are not individually mandatory keywords.

4. Record issues in Issues

Add one row per distinct issue group, starting at row 2. If you find no issues, leave this sheet without a row for that report; completion is recorded in Review. Do not add a placeholder error.

issue_idUse a unique ID within that report, for example I001, I002.
decisionError, Acceptable, Pending adjudication or Outside case-error scope.

5. Add the two control flags only where applicable

For each issue row on a control report, fill both pathology_flag and normality_flag with Yes or No. These annotate an existing Error or Pending adjudication issue; they are not extra errors or penalties.

  • pathology_flag: unsupported disease, pathological deficit/mechanism or clinical impairment.
  • normality_flag: unsupported normal physiology, timing/function or overall normal gait.

Yes on a pending issue marks its potentially qualifying meaning; it does not turn that issue into a confirmed error. Acceptable and Outside case-error scope rows use No for both flags. For stroke rows, leave these two fields blank or select No.

Do not mark a flag merely because a report states control membership, a displayed reference comparison or a qualified open assessment question. Judge a question that presupposes a deficit under the existing error rules. The later report-level aggregation keeps confirmed, pending and negative states separate; you do not need to calculate it.

6. Mark that report completed in Review

Complete columns D-H. Set review_state = Completed and issue_review_complete = Yes after reading the whole report and recording any issues. Yes means the issue search is complete, not that the report is error-free. This can coexist with documented Pending judgments.

Record four_section_format separately using Yes, No or Cannot assess. Record incidental_unblinding as Yes or No; if the text explicitly reveals a model identity or identity becomes known, describe the circumstance in notes. Do not search for the model or edit the report. A stylistic guess is not a confirmed identity.

If review materials are missing or mismatched, document the problem and request the correct copy. Do not silently score a missing file as a model omission. A short or incomplete captured report remains assessable as the actual output.

7. Check and return your completed workbook

After all 105 reports, check all 552 coverage rows and finish the Instructions sheet: reviewer name (B15), completion date (B16, ISO YYYY-MM-DD as text), and independent review complete (B17: Yes). If your editor converts the date into a date object, prefix it with an apostrophe to keep text. Do not enter a name, completion date or Yes until you have completed your independent review.

Return only your saved Independent_ratings_rater2_completed.xlsx, unless you need to attach a separate material-problem note. Keep pending judgments with their reasons for later discussion. Do not send or request the other reviewer's ratings before both independent submissions are locked.
