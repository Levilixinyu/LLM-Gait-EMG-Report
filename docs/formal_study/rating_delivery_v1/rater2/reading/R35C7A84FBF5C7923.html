## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

**How these numbers must be read.** The released curves are **ensemble-averaged envelopes**, normalized using a shared maximum across available strides before averaging (strides are not individually rescaled). Amplitudes are therefore **relative envelope values** — not neural activation, force or strength — and muscles may not be ranked as stronger or weaker from them. An averaged curve cannot demonstrate stride-to-stride variability, and the pattern described below must not be assumed to occur in every individual stride. Detected intervals are properties of the averaged envelope under the stated rule (**>20% of that curve's own peak, minimum retained interval 3% of cycle**) and are not physiological onset, offset, contraction duration or inactivity. Where an interval begins or ends at 0%/100%, that is the analysis-window boundary (initial contact of contributing strides), not a physiological event.
