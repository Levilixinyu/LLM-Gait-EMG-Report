# Revised scoring examples

All 42 examples are newly authored teaching examples, not actual model responses or expert judgments. All decisions remain proposed until this rubric version is confirmed. Case-specific numbers apply only to their identified input.

## X01: G03

> The reference is not individually matched, so these differences cannot isolate a stroke effect.

**Proposed assessment:** Covered

Both meanings are present; no age/speed word checklist.

## X02: G03

> The reference is pooled.

**Proposed assessment:** Not covered in isolation

A source label alone conveys neither required limitation. With one meaningful limitation supplied elsewhere, the combined report may be Partially covered.

## X03: G03

> These comparisons cannot separate slow-speed and stroke effects. Later: the deviation proves stroke-specific dysfunction.

**Proposed assessment:** Covered plus separate error

First statement conveys both meanings; assess the later unsupported assertion separately.

## X04: Sub09 C04

> ST and VL each have max |z| 4.2; at those locations, both envelopes are above their phase-specific reference means. Their muscle-specific percentile classification is indeterminate from the supplied endpoints.

**Proposed assessment:** Covered

Sub09 C04: both complete numerical routes; cycle locations are optional for this item. Standardized magnitude is syntactically separate from above-mean envelope direction.

## X05: Sub09 C04

> There is insufficient information to classify EMG normality.

**Proposed assessment:** Not covered

Correct general caution does not describe the required ST/VL findings.

## X06: Sub09 C04

> ST and VL have broad detected intervals extending across the initial-contact boundary, approximately 83 to 44% and 88 to 48%.

**Proposed assessment:** Covered

Complete interval alternative; numerical z route is not additionally required.

## X07: Sub05 C04

> ST and VL are elevated relative to the mean reference at 35% and 5%.

**Proposed assessment:** Partially covered plus E01

ST is correct; VL direction is wrong. Distinguish coverage of the components from the erroneous assertion.

## X08: Sub05 TA optional

> The displayed TA max |z| is 4.6, below the displayed 4.61 endpoint; this does not settle its unrounded classification or clinical normality.

**Proposed assessment:** Acceptable optional description

Visible-number comparison only; no omission penalty if TA is not discussed.

## X09: Sub50 ST

Do not require a hidden ST-specific percentile. In isolation this optional comparison does not fully cover Sub50 C03: above-mean direction and approximate location are still needed.

## X15: Sub05 TA event anchor

## X16: Sub05 GAS phase and anatomy

**Proposed assessment:** E04

The supplied Sub05 location is a maximum standardized difference, not an interval or phase. Anatomical naming is allowed; the inferred phase function is not.

## X17: Sub47 C03

## X18: Sub47 C03

No recordings is not physiological inactivity. The ambiguous phrase no EMG activity was measured does not itself establish coverage of missing recordings; inspect the whole report.

If speed/cadence/symmetry are not discussed, no compulsory missing-field inventory is added.

## X27: Sub05 VL explicit correction

## X28: Mixed design and case assertion

**Proposed assessment:** Design concern plus E09; cross-linked

Separate the universal design guarantee from the participant-specific causal assertion. Do not exclude the latter because it shares a sentence.
