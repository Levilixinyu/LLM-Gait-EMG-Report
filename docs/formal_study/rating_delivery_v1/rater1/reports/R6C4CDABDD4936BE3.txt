**1. Objective Findings**
This report details findings for subject Sub07, a participant in the healthy control group. Because the source aggregates and averages left and right strides for controls, an affected-side designation is not applicable, nor is the paretic/non-paretic step-length symmetry index. Gait speed and cadence are not provided. 

Available objective data include sagittal plane kinematics (ankle, knee, hip, pelvis) and normalized mean EMG envelopes for the gastrocnemius (GAS), rectus femoris (RF), vastus lateralis (VL), biceps femoris (BF), semitendinosus (ST), tibialis anterior (TA), and erector spinae (ERS). Coronal and transverse plane kinematics were not supplied. 

Comparisons are made against a leave-one-out reference of 137 pooled healthy subjects. Because the participant’s specific gait speed is unavailable, it cannot be determined whether a speed-matched reference exists. Furthermore, membership in a healthy cohort does not establish that all underlying individual physiology is strictly normal, and deviations from this empirical reference do not by themselves establish clinical pathology or impairment.

**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG interval findings are based on a computational threshold (regions where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle length). Amplitude values refer to relative mean envelope height and do not measure absolute muscle force or strength. The 0% and 100% boundaries represent the initial contact of the contributing strides. 

*   **GAS**: Retained an interval from 12% to 47% of the cycle, resulting in 36% coverage. The peak normalized amplitude was 0.65.
*   **RF**: Retained intervals from 47% to 71% and from 83% to 22%. The latter interval remains above threshold across the initial-contact boundary. Total coverage was 64% of the cycle, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.65.
*   **VL**: Retained intervals from 41% to 71% and from 80% to 21% (wrapping across the initial-contact boundary). Total coverage was 72%, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.46.
*   **BF**: Retained intervals from 16% to 30% and from 79% to 3% (wrapping the cycle boundary). Total coverage was 39%, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.76.
*   **ST**: Retained intervals from 13% to 38% and from 79% to 10% (wrapping the cycle boundary). Total coverage was 57%, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.72.
*   **TA**: Retained intervals from 54% to 82% and from 86% to 29% (wrapping the cycle boundary). Total coverage was 71%, with a peak normalized amplitude of 0.67.
*   **ERS**: Retained a nearly continuous interval from 86% to 84% wrapping across the initial-contact boundary, resulting in 98% coverage of the analysis cycle. The peak normalized amplitude was 0.70.

The Falconer-Winter Co-Contraction Index (CCI) calculates the full-cycle overlap of normalized EMG envelopes. The TA vs. GAS CCI was 0.39, matching the reference median of 0.39 and falling within the supplied 2.5th–97.5th percentile empirical interval (0.24–0.59). The RF vs. BF CCI was 0.57, which is below the dataset reference median of 0.68 but still within the empirical interval (0.48–0.83). Note that CCI reflects computational overlap of normalized envelopes and does not establish a physiological clinical classification of co-activation.

**3. Kinematic and Reference Comparisons**
Kinematic deviations and maximum |z|-scores track differences from the reference cohort. The maximum |z|-score describes the single largest standardized difference from the phase-specific healthy mean; it does not identify the peak amplitude time or classify abnormality, as extreme values above 2.0 occur routinely in the healthy reference dataset.

*   **Composite Kinematics**: The composite kinematic deviation score (GPS-like) was 2.55, which is below the reference median of 5.1 and 95th percentile of 10.7. 
*   **Sagittal Joint Metrics**: Ankle range of motion (ROM) was 21.7° with a local Gait Variable Score (GVS) of 3.10. Knee ROM was 60.3° (GVS 3.13), hip ROM was 43.0° (GVS 2.15), and pelvis ROM was 1.6° (GVS 1.44). Without a matched reference, ROM values do not inherently establish mobility restriction or excess. 
*   **EMG Maximum |z|-Scores**: The most pronounced deviation from the phase-specific reference mean occurred in the ST (max |z| = 4.1 at 27% of the cycle, above the reference mean). Other maximum |z|-scores included RF (2.8 at 53%), ERS (2.7 at 69%), BF (2.7 at 90%), VL (2.4 at 49%), TA (2.0 at 75%), and GAS (1.6 at 29%); in all instances, the subject's envelope was above the phase-specific reference mean at those locations.
