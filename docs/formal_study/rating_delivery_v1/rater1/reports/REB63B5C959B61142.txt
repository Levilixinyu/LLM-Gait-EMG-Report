**Co-contraction Indices (CCI):**
CCI is a whole-cycle overlap index calculated using the continuous normalized envelopes. 
*   **TA vs GAS:** CCI = 0.59. This value lies exactly at the upper boundary of the supplied healthy empirical interval (0.24-0.59; median 0.39).

*   *Note:* Falling within or near an endpoint of these reference intervals does not by itself establish physiologically normal co-activation, nor does it establish pathological control or stiffness. 

### 3. Kinematic and Reference Comparisons
*Comparability Caveat:* Comparisons are relative to a pooled cohort of 138 healthy subjects whose self-selected walking speeds span 0.80-1.79 m/s. The participant's gait speed (0.24 m/s) falls below this support limit, meaning no speed-matched healthy reference exists in this dataset. Observed deviations from the reference conflate the effects of slow walking with stroke-specific control changes, and the two cannot be separated from these data. These comparisons alone do not establish a clinical diagnosis.
