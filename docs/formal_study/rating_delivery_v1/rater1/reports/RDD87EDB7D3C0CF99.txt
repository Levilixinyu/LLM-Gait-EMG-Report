**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
EMG findings are derived from ensemble-averaged envelopes normalized to their own peak across available strides. Envelope amplitudes are relative values and do not represent absolute muscular force, strength, or stride-to-stride variability. Above-threshold intervals indicate periods where the mean envelope exceeded 20% of its own peak for at least 3% of the cycle, not physiological onsets, offsets, or absolute inactivity outside these boundaries.
