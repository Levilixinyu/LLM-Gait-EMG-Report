## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

The released envelopes were normalized using a shared maximum across available strides for each participant, side, and muscle before averaging, without further amplitude normalization here. These relative amplitudes are **not measures of strength or force** and do not establish stride-to-stride variability.

Retained intervals identify where the **averaged envelope exceeded 20% of its own peak**, subject to a minimum interval of **3% of the cycle by sample count**. They do not identify physiological onset, offset, continuous contraction, or inactivity outside the intervals.
