All seven averaged envelopes are explicitly reported as remaining above threshold across the **100%→0% initial-contact cycle boundary**. These intervals and coverage values do not establish physiological onset, offset, continuous contraction, or inactivity elsewhere, nor do they demonstrate the same pattern in every stride. Without individual intermediate events or matched muscle-specific timing references, no gait-phase or normal/early/delayed timing interpretation is supported.

At each listed maximum-|z| location, the envelope is **above the phase-specific reference mean**. These locations identify the largest standardized reference differences, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or gait events. TA **5.9** and ERS **8.2** exceed their supplied pooled healthy 95th-percentile benchmarks of **4.61** and **3.65**, respectively. These are descriptive extreme-value comparisons, not diagnostic classifications; a conventional single-observation \|z\| = 2 cutoff is inappropriate.

**Whole-cycle CCI:**
- **TA–GAS: 0.40**, within the supplied empirical interval **0.24–0.59**, and close to the supplied median **0.39**.
- **RF–BF: 0.54**, within **0.48–0.83**, below the supplied median **0.68** and nearer the lower endpoint.

CCI measures overlap of the full normalized envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, muscular force, or a phase-specific result. These within-interval values do not establish physiologically normal co-activation or muscle function, and their interpretation remains dependent on the normalization and envelope methods.
