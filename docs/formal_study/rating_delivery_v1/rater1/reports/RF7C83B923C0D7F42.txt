## 1. Objective Findings

Sub73 is a **control participant**; affected side is **not applicable**. Control-group membership alone does not establish normal physiology.

The release documents pooling of left- and right-side strides. Accordingly, these findings describe a pooled cycle, not a named limb. The 0%/100% boundary represents initial contact of contributing strides; no intermediate gait events are supplied.

Available findings include seven EMG mean envelopes and four sagittal-plane kinematic channels. Coronal/transverse kinematics are not included. Gait speed and cadence are not provided. The task’s paretic/non-paretic step-length index is not applicable; this does not establish left/right symmetry.

## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

Retained intervals identify where the **ensemble-averaged envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak**, with a minimum retained interval of 3% of the cycle by sample count. They are not physiological onset/offset measurements, continuous-contraction durations, or evidence of inactivity outside the intervals. They also do not establish the pattern in individual strides.

Normalization uses a shared maximum across available strides before averaging, without further amplitude normalization in this pipeline. Peak values are relative envelope amplitudes—not strength, force, or absolute neural activation.

No stance/swing assignment or normal, early, delayed or prolonged timing characterization is supported without individual events and matched muscle-specific timing references.

Maximum |z| denotes the largest standardized difference across 1001 cycle points, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at the listed location, not a healthy percentile limit. These extreme-value statistics should not be classified using a conventional |z| = 2 cutoff.

**Whole-cycle CCI comparisons:**

These are Falconer–Winter overlap indices of the full normalized envelopes, including subthreshold contributions. They are not simultaneous-activation time, muscular force or phase-specific findings. Their values depend on normalization and envelope processing; interval membership does not establish physiologically normal co-activation or muscle function.

The **GPS-like score is 5.47**, calculated as the RMS of the supplied sagittal GVS values—not the validated nine-variable GPS. It is above the supplied pooled calibration median **5.1** and below its **95th percentile of 10.7**.

The actual deviation reference comprises **137 healthy subjects excluding Sub73**, evaluated at **1001 phase points**, from `main_cohort_excel`. The supplied calibration percentiles are not matched leave-one-out normal limits. Reference comparability remains limited: the broader cohort pools sides and ages, and subject-specific speed matching cannot be assessed. These comparisons, including the EMG and CCI comparisons, cannot establish a disease effect, clinical diagnosis or normal physiology.
