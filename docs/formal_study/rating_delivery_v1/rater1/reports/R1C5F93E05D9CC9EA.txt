**2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings**
The following EMG findings represent averaged envelopes normalized to each muscle's respective maximum amplitude across the available strides. Above-threshold intervals are regions where the mean envelope exceeds 20% of its peak for a minimum of 3% of the analysis cycle (0% to 100%, anchored at the initial contact of contributing strides). These parameters describe the algorithmic average and do not establish precise physiological muscle onsets, offsets, continuous contraction duration, or complete inactivity outside the detected intervals.
