These findings do not establish continuous physiological contraction or the same pattern in every stride. Other percentages cannot be assigned to gait phases from this summary.

Maximum |z| identifies the **largest standardized reference difference**, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude. “Above” and “below” refer to the phase-specific reference mean at the listed location. This extreme-value statistic should not be classified using a conventional |z| > 2 rule. TA 3.9 and ERS 3.5 are numerically below their supplied pooled 95th-percentile benchmarks of 4.61 and 3.65, respectively; these comparisons do not establish normal muscle function.

**Whole-cycle envelope overlap:**

- **TA–GAS CCI: 0.62**, above the supplied empirical interval of 0.24–0.59; median 0.39.
- **RF–BF CCI: 0.68**, equal to the supplied median and within the empirical interval of 0.48–0.83.

CCI summarizes overlap of the full normalized envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, force or a phase-specific result, and depends on normalization and envelope methods. These values alone do not establish pathological or physiologically normal co-activation.
