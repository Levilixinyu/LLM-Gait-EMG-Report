## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

The retained intervals describe where the **ensemble-averaged envelope exceeds 20% of its own peak**, with a minimum retained interval of 3% of the cycle by sample count. They do not establish physiological onset, offset, contraction duration, inactivity outside the intervals or the pattern in every stride.

Released normalization uses a participant-, side- and muscle-specific maximum shared across available strides before averaging, with no further amplitude normalization. These relative amplitudes do not measure strength or force.

The maximum-|z| location identifies the **largest standardized difference from the phase-specific reference mean**, not necessarily peak envelope amplitude or a gait event. “Above” and “below” in the table describe direction relative to that mean, not a percentile limit.
