## 2. EMG Envelope and Threshold Findings

Retained intervals reflect the **averaged envelope exceeding 20% of its own peak**, with a minimum retained duration of 3% of the cycle by sample count. They are not physiological activation onsets, offsets or contraction durations, and do not establish inactivity elsewhere or the same pattern in every stride.

Normalization used a shared maximum across available strides for each participant-side-muscle combination, without independently rescaling each stride or further normalizing the released mean curve. The denominator was not shared between sides. Peak values therefore describe relative envelope amplitude—not force, strength or absolute neural activation.

**Whole-cycle envelope overlap:**

CCI quantifies whole-cycle overlap of normalized continuous envelopes, including sub-threshold contributions. It is not simultaneous activation time, force or a phase-specific finding, and depends on normalization and envelope methods. These comparisons do not establish pathological co-activation, stiffness or impaired control.
