# Selected reporting topics and manuscript locations

This working crosswalk uses the transparency topics discussed in the [CHART explanation and elaboration](https://www.bmj.com/content/390/bmj-2024-083305). It is not the completed official checklist or a statement of journal compliance. The study evaluates constrained report interpretation, with diagnosis and treatment outside scope. Authors should select the journal and complete its applicable checklist before submission.

| Topic | Present location | Remaining author action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective and study scope | Abstract; Introduction; Methods: study design | Confirm final framing and target article type |
| Dataset, eligibility and sample size | Methods: data source and case selection; Table 1 | Confirm secondary-analysis institutional ethics determination |
| Saved model identity and configuration | Methods: model configuration; Table S1 | Confirm identifiers are reported as historical aliases, not immutable weights |
| Model-visible inputs and boundaries | Methods: feature definitions; saved plan and case prompts | Confirm permitted release of exact input material |
| Query strategy and generation | Methods: generation; recorded operational stages | Preserve single-output design, dates and zero-retry accounting |
| Human evaluation | Methods: expert workflow; tables S4-S5; original agreement | Confirmed: three UT Austin PhD students with at least five years in EMG analysis and neuromuscular control research; no clinical licensure inferred |
| Amendments and exposure | Methods: workflow changes; timeline S4; targeted sensitivity | Retain post-independent and post-unblinding timing |
| Endpoints and statistical methods | Methods: outcomes; main tables; joint denominator column | Do not convert descriptive sensitivity into a new confirmatory claim |
| Results and uncertainty | Tables 1-6 and S1-S6; separate control analysis | Review narrative against exact table values |
| Discussion and limitations | Discussion; Limitations; Conclusion | Select journal-specific length and reference style |
| Reproducibility and availability | Offline bundle README, validation record, source map | Approve final public release/license and permitted sharing of expert records |
| Declarations | Author confirmation checklist | Complete affiliations, contributions, funding, conflicts and applicable assistance disclosures |

The portable bundle has been prepared for local researcher use. Availability statements must not imply that private files are already in a public clone or that source-data replay includes a fresh raw-signal audit. Original independent scoring and later human reconsideration remain identifiable stages.
