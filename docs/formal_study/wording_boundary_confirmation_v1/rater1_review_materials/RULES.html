D03: questions and assertions

Interpret clinical follow-up questions as possibilities to be verified, not established mechanisms or diagnoses. Mentioning spasticity, compensation, instability, or a phase-related clinical task in a question is not itself an error. Separately inspect independently checkable false facts and explicit assertions that an abnormal finding has already been observed. This boundary preserves the existing factual-error rule; it is not an additional user signature.

Control annotation

For an unsupported pathological/clinical-impairment inference, use the human reviewer's explicit semantic judgment and pathology annotation. A simultaneous E02 category tag is not required.

  • No flag is inferred solely from a question, E07, a numerical deviation, or healthy-group membership.
  • A confirmed in-scope issue and explicit expert annotation are required for a positive confirmed pathology outcome.
  • Pending and missing annotations remain subject to the approved uncertainty rules.
  • Preserve all personal category choices and original independent scores.

Decision options

  • Error: identify the actual unsupported or false assertion and its applicable rule.
  • Acceptable: explain why the passage, read in context, does not make that prohibited assertion.
  • Pending adjudication: preserve competing readings when the existing rule and context do not resolve them. This is not a confirmed error.

Missing or mismatched files are a material problem, not interpretive Pending. Record them in Completion rather than guessing a decision.

Threshold evidence: a scope check, not an answer key

Reported 100% coverage, a displayed 0%-100% retained interval and supplied boundary continuity may be described. The stronger claim that no sample fell below threshold is different. Even these three displayed fields together need not guarantee that stronger claim: on a synthetic 1001-sample curve, two dips at indices 1 and 999 leave a long run 0.2%-99.8% (displayed 0%-100%), coverage 99.8002% (displayed 100%) and both boundary samples above threshold. The short two-sample boundary run is discarded by the ABOVE-threshold duration rule.

This is an assistant-checked logical example, not participant data, a new expert-approved rule or evidence that any reviewed participant had dips. Still decide whether the actual report asserts a stronger guarantee or merely describes the supplied output. Boundary continuity does not by itself assert physiological contraction.
