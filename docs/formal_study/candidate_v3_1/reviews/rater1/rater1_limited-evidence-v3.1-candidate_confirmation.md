## Required points

CCI interpretation: **Agree**

R01 and R10 are resolved. The instruction explicitly separates empirical interval membership from physiologically normal co-activation or normal muscle function, and separates values outside the interval from pathology, stiffness, impaired control and diagnosis. Whole-cycle scope, normalization dependence and the prohibition on importing thresholds from other algorithms are clear. Comparisons with supplied medians and endpoints remain available. This is a prospective wording decision and does not reopen Q055.

Stroke normalization and healthy verification scope: **Agree**

R02 and R03 are resolved. The stroke input specifies participant-, side- and muscle-specific denominators and distinguishes 614 verified evaluable combinations from 86 unevaluable combinations. The healthy input explicitly identifies documentary provenance and does not extend the stroke numerical audit to healthy pooling or denominators. The mean-envelope detection threshold is separately defined as 20% of that curve's own peak.

Stroke initial-contact boundary: **Agree**

R06 is resolved for the stroke cases. The dictionary supports successive ipsilateral initial contacts as the endpoints of the analyzed paretic stride. Calling the boundary initial contact, or stating numerical proximity to it, is justified. Successive contacts are not the same physical instant. This anchor does not provide toe-off, intermediate phase boundaries, matched normal timing or physiological EMG onset/offset. The supplied consistency record reports no invalid interval in the four reviewed stroke cases. The separate Sub43 flag does not block confirmation of these instructions and remains a future eligibility-review issue.

Healthy pooled initial-contact boundary with documentary qualification: **Agree**

R06 is resolved for control Sub49. The dictionary's side-based segmentation and the healthy Readme's averaging of left/right strides support the stated documentary interpretation. Describing the boundary as initial contact of contributing strides appropriately avoids identifying the pooled curve as one named limb's observed event trace. This confirmation does not establish numerical verification of healthy MAT aggregation.

Anatomical actions versus gait-phase functions: **Agree**

R07 is resolved. Naming an anatomical action does not itself establish that the muscle performed a particular task during the reported interval. The revised rule permits useful anatomical context while retaining the evidence requirement for clearance, push-off, weight acceptance and other phase-specific functions. No supported case-specific phase interpretation is lost under the supplied inputs.

Speed-band labels and group-specific field applicability: **Agree**

R04 and R05 are resolved. Each supplied stroke speed label is explicitly a speed band rather than measured independence, safety or real-world mobility. The input distinguishes stroke-specific fields from information potentially available for either group. R13 correctly separates missing control cadence from the non-applicability of this task's paretic/non-paretic index; it does not deny the possibility of a general left/right symmetry measure.

Other revisions R08-R14: **Agree**

R08 preserves descriptions of mean-envelope coverage, retained intervals and boundary continuity without converting these into physiological duration or a pattern established for every stride. Rounded coverage alone cannot establish the absence of a below-threshold segment. R09 separates the location of maximum standardized deviation from an amplitude peak or event, and its signed direction from a percentile comparison. R11 makes the preparation list explicitly non-exhaustive. R12 permits a sufficient, brief missing-EMG section for Sub47. R14 preserves supported numerical comparisons while respecting supplied precision and the model-visible information boundary.

The substantive reference-comparability requirement remains intact: both limited comparability and inability to establish disease effects or diagnosis must be conveyed. Separate recitation of age and speed is unnecessary; an in-range speed does not establish matching. Controls remain subject to these limits.
